Why gun cases and safes get musty

A gun case or safe is closed up for long stretches. Damp air gets in when the lid is opened, then stays trapped. In humid coastal areas this can lead to mould in gun safes, musty smells and mildew on case linings and any cloth or leather parts.

Stop Mildew is a 92% paraformaldehyde mildew inhibitor. Its vapour stops the damaging mould growth that causes mildew. Gun and ammunition cases are one of the places it is made for, along with other closed storage like trunks and lockers.

How to use Stop Mildew in a gun case or safe

Use 28 g (one pouch) for every 2.9 m³ of closed storage space. Here is a worked example. A gun safe measuring 0.6 m × 0.5 m × 1.5 m holds about 0.45 m³, so one pouch is plenty. A large walk-in safe of about 5.8 m³ would need two pouches.

  • Place the pouch inside the case or safe.
  • Keep the case lid or safe door shut. The pouch only works in a closed space.
  • One pouch lasts 3–6 months.
  • When the pouch is empty, swap in a fresh one.

An honest note about plastic cases

Stop Mildew is not for storing film or articles made of plastic. Many modern gun cases are moulded plastic. If yours is, check this before you use a pouch. If you are unsure about your case, ask us before you buy.

Owner to confirm: suitability for gun finishes, ammunition and polymer cases.

Things to watch

  • Everything must be completely dry before it is exposed to Stop Mildew. Wipe down and dry your gear first.
  • Before working in the case or safe, remove the pouch and keep the case or safe open for several hours to air it.
  • Never place a pouch in refrigerators or food containers.
  • Follow the safe storage rules that apply to firearms and ammunition where you live.
