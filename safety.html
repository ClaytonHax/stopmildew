Using Stop Mildew safely

Stop Mildew is a 92% paraformaldehyde mildew inhibitor. Its vapour stops the damaging mould growth that causes mildew. Use it as described below, and keep to the warnings.

What is in the pouch

The active ingredient is paraformaldehyde, at 92%. The pouch releases a vapour that fills the closed space. That is why the space must stay closed, and why the dosage matters: one 28 g pouch for every 2.9 m³. For example, a 5.8 m³ space needs two pouches.

Where it is meant to be used

Stop Mildew is made for closets and wardrobes, trunks and chests, gym lockers and hampers, shoe shelves, glassed-in bookcases, boat hatches, rope and sail lockers, gun and ammunition cases, fishing bags and tackle boxes, and cellars and attics. These are all closed-up spaces. It is not for storing film or articles made of plastic, and it never goes in refrigerators or food containers.

How to use it

  • Hang the pouch in a closet, or place it in a trunk, chest or locker.
  • Use 28 g (one pouch) for every 2.9 m³ of closed storage space.
  • Keep closet doors and container lids shut.
  • One pouch lasts 3–6 months. When the pouch is empty, swap in a fresh one.

Four warnings

  • Garments must be completely dry before exposure to Stop Mildew.
  • Before working in a closet, remove the pouch and keep the door open for several hours to air it.
  • Not for storing film or articles made of plastic.
  • Never place in refrigerators or food containers.

For example, if you are going to sort or work in your wardrobe, take the pouch out first and leave the door open. See mildew in wardrobes for more on closets.
