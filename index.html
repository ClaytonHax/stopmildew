What is it?

A simple pouch that keeps mould away.

Stop Mildew is a 92% paraformaldehyde mildew inhibitor. Its vapour stops the damaging mould growth that causes mildew.

Each draw-string pouch protects the things you store from harmful mould, mildew and musty, stale odours.

  • Clothing
  • Leather goods
  • Books
  • Paper & documents

How to use it

Three steps. Months of protection.

  1. Hang or place

    Hang the pouch in a closet or place it in a trunk, chest or locker. Use 28 g (one pouch) for every 2.9 m³ of closed storage space.

  2. Keep it closed

    Keep closet doors and container lids shut. One pouch lasts 3–6 months.

  3. Replace

    When the pouch is empty, swap in a fresh one for continued protection.

Please note

  • Garments must be completely dry before exposure to Stop Mildew.
  • Before working in a closet, remove the pouch and keep the door open for several hours to air it.
  • Not for storing film or articles made of plastic.
  • Never place in refrigerators or food containers.
