Mildew in wardrobes: how to stop it

Keep clothes, leather and shoes fresh in closed-up cupboards in humid coastal homes. Hang a pouch and shut the door.

Why wardrobes and cupboards get mouldy

A wardrobe is closed up most of the day. In humid coastal homes, like those along the KZN coast, that shut-in space is where mould and mildew can take hold. You open the door and get a musty, stale smell on your clothes, leather and shoes.

Stop Mildew is made to protect everything stored inside a closed wardrobe or cupboard, so you do not have to keep checking on it.

How Stop Mildew protects your wardrobe

Stop Mildew is a 92% paraformaldehyde mildew inhibitor. Its vapour stops the damaging mould growth that causes mildew. It comes in draw-string pouches that protect clothing and leather goods from mould, mildew and musty, stale odours.

There is no beating, no water tray to empty and no fuss. You hang a pouch and close the door.

How to use it in a wardrobe or cupboard

  • Hang the pouch inside the closet.
  • Use 28 g for every 2.9 m³ of closed storage space. A cupboard of 5.8 m³ needs two pouches.
  • Keep the doors shut. A pouch lasts 3–6 months.
  • When the pouch is empty, swap in a fresh one.

Stop Mildew also works in trunks and chests, gym lockers and hampers and on shoe shelves, so you can protect shoes and spare bedding in the same way. Not sure how many pouches you need? Our Stop Mildew FAQ has a worked example.

Before you hang the pouch

  • Garments must be completely dry before exposure to Stop Mildew.
  • Before working in a closet, remove the pouch and keep the door open for several hours to air it.
  • Not for storing film or articles made of plastic.
  • Never place in refrigerators or food containers.
