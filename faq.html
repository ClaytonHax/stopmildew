Answers about Stop Mildew pouches

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. For the full picture, see how to use it on the homepage.

How many Stop Mildew pouches do I need?

Use one 28 g pouch for every 2.9 m³ of closed storage space. For example, a 5.8 m³ space needs two pouches, and a 2.9 m³ space needs one.

How long does one pouch last?

One pouch lasts 3–6 months. When the pouch is empty, swap in a fresh one for continued protection.

How do I use Stop Mildew?

Hang the pouch in a closet, or place it in a trunk, chest or locker. Keep closet doors and container lids shut. When the pouch is empty, replace it.

What is Stop Mildew made of?

Stop Mildew is a 92% paraformaldehyde mildew inhibitor. Its vapour stops the damaging mould growth that causes mildew.

Where should I not use Stop Mildew?

Do not use it for storing film or articles made of plastic. Never place it in refrigerators or food containers.

More detail is on our safety page.

Must my clothes be dry, and do I need to air the closet first?

Garments must be completely dry before exposure to Stop Mildew. Before working in a closet, remove the pouch and keep the door open for several hours to air it.
